What's coming

THE EARTH MOVIE premieres here, Summer 2027

A satirical feature film about a backwards world where everyone is hooked on their feeds and scored for everything they do. One guy wakes up. What follows is a wild trip through the noise to find the people who are still real.

Underneath the jokes is a simple lesson: love and kindness are worth more than money or stuff. Family friendly, no cussing, with grown-up jokes older viewers will catch.

Made with AI. EARTH uses AI-generated characters, voices and imagery, directed and produced by Zach Wennstedt at Madwackd Productions.
